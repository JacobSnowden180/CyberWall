About

People, not a plugin you install and forget

CyberWall is a mostly human security service. People do the watching, the deciding, and the writing. Monitoring tools and standard checks help them keep up. We do not claim the work is free of automation, and we do not claim it is a guarantee.

Why this exists

CyberWall is founder-led. The founder follows vulnerability news and wants small stores to get a careful fix, explained in plain English, including on weeks when the hole is in PHP itself rather than in a plugin.

The aim is to notice relevant news early, sometimes before the major public trackers list it. That is a goal. It is not a promise that we will always hear first.

Large companies have security staff. A small shop often has a plugin and some hope. The bet behind CyberWall is that those shops should be able to talk to a person who will not make them feel foolish for asking.

Help small online businesses stay safer by fixing known security holes quickly, carefully, and in plain English.

What we value

Plain language

If we cannot explain it simply, we have not explained it. Jargon gets a plain version beside it.

Safe changes

Test first, check checkout, keep a way back. Careful beats fast when customers are paying.

No scare tactics

We will say what is real and what it means for a shop. We will not invent urgency to sell a plan.

How the work is split

People make the decisions. Monitoring tools help us keep watch, and standard checks help us test, the way a careful trade uses good instruments.

On Priority, a person reviews every update before it goes live. On Essentials and Standard, the team reviews and monitors, routine updates use standard checks, and a person reviews a change that is large or that touches payment.
