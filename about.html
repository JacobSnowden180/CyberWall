About CyberWall

A human-run team that looks after your store's security

CyberWall is a mostly-human security service: our people do the watching, the deciding and the writing, supported by monitoring tools to help us keep up.

Why we exist

CyberWall is founder-led. Our founder follows vulnerability news closely and aims to learn about new security holes as they emerge, sometimes before the major public trackers list them, and to turn that into fixes quickly.

Big companies have security teams. Small shops usually have a plugin and some hope. We think small businesses deserve real people on their side, who explain things in plain language and don't make them feel silly for asking.

Our mission

Help small online businesses stay safe by fixing known security holes quickly, carefully and in plain English.

What we value

Plain language

If we can't explain it simply, we haven't explained it well. No jargon without a plain explanation.

Safe changes

We test first, check your checkout, and keep a way back. Careful beats fast when your customers are paying.

No scare tactics

We'll tell you what's real and what matters, and we won't invent urgency to sell you something.

How we work

People make the decisions and do the work at CyberWall. We use monitoring tools to help us keep watch and standard checks to help us test, the same way any careful professional uses good equipment. When it matters, a person looks at it.

On our Priority plan, a real person reviews every update. On other plans, our team reviews and monitors, and routine updates are handled with our standard tools and checks.
