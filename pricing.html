Questions people ask

Are these prices final?

No. They are proposed for early access and may change. Requesting early access does not lock a price. Nothing is charged on this website.

What is “staging”, in plain words?

A private copy of your store that customers cannot see. Each fix is tried there first. If something goes wrong, the real store is not the thing that breaks.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We do the technical work and explain it in plain English. If we need something from you, we say exactly what and how.

What hosting do you work with?

The service is built for WooCommerce, WordPress, Magento, and Shopware. Hosting setups vary, so during early access we check yours with you before you start. Sometimes the PHP update itself has to be done by the hosting company. In that case we tell you what to ask them for, and we test the store on the new version first.

What if an update would break my store?

That is why we test on a copy first. If a fix causes trouble there, we do not put it on the live store. We look for a safer way, and a person tells you. If something unexpected happens after a live update, a rollback is ready.

Do you guarantee I will not be hacked?

No, and no one honestly can. We reduce risk by fixing known holes quickly and carefully, with checks along the way.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are month to month. The written cancellation terms are not published yet. They will be, before anyone is billed. Privacy and terms status.

Where does my data live?

The aim is to keep the private test copy in a private, access-controlled place, and to remove it when you leave. The exact details will be confirmed in writing before we take on customers. A privacy policy is not published yet.

What do you need access to?

Enough access to the store and hosting to make a test copy and apply updates. We agree that up front, ask only for what we need, and explain why. We never ask you to email a password.

Is there a faster or slower reply time?

Standard is meant to be answered ahead of Essentials, and Priority ahead of Standard. The actual target times are not decided, so they are not printed. We will not invent a number of hours.

Is CyberWall open to everyone yet?

No. Early access is for a small number of stores, so each one can be looked after properly. Joining the request list does not commit you.
