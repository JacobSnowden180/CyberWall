How we compare

Many self-serve security tools cost roughly $10–$50 a month, but they mostly scan or block rather than safely fix. Hands-on managed Magento patching often runs $300–$1,200 a month. CyberWall sits in between: real people who fix things safely, at a small-business price.

Ranges are rough, general market estimates, not quotes from any named company.

Questions people ask

What is “staging”, in plain words?

It's a private copy of your store that only we can see. We try each fix there first, so if something goes wrong, your real store and real customers are never affected.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We do the technical work and explain it in plain English. If we need something from you, we'll tell you exactly what and how.

What hosting do you work with?

We're built for stores on WooCommerce, WordPress, Magento and Shopware. Hosting setups vary a lot, so during early access we'll check yours with you before you sign up. Sometimes the PHP update itself has to be done by your hosting company. In that case we'll tell you exactly what to ask them for, and test your store on the new version first.

What if an update would break my store?

That's the whole reason we test on a copy first. If a fix causes trouble there, we don't put it on your live store. We work out a safer way, and a person on our team will let you know. And if something unexpected happens after a live update, a rollback is ready.

Do you guarantee I won't be hacked?

No, and no one honestly can. What we do is reduce the risk by fixing known security holes quickly and safely, with checks along the way.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are month to month.

Where does my data live?

Our aim is to keep your private test copy in a private, access-controlled place, and to remove it when you leave. We'll confirm the exact details in writing, and in our privacy policy, before we take on customers. (Privacy policy: coming soon.)

What do you need access to?

Enough access to your store and hosting to make a test copy and apply updates, which we'll agree with you up front. We'll ask only for what we need, and we'll explain why. We never ask you to email us passwords.

Is CyberWall open to everyone yet?

Not yet. We're launching with early access and a waitlist, so we can look after each store properly. Joining early access doesn't commit you to anything.

Want a real person looking after your store's security?

We're opening early access to a small number of stores. Tell us a little about yours and a member of our team will get back to you.
